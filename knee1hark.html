Gentle Yoga for Knee Pain

A complete, gentle routine that strengthens the muscles around the knee and eases stiffness.

5 poses · 15–20 minutes · mat, block, strap

Next step: Roll out a mat and follow the 5 poses in order. Save this page for your next practice.
Start the routine

Before you start

Set up in two minutes, then warm up.

  • Mat and a folded blanketTo pad your knees
  • Yoga block and a strap nearbyA thick book and a towel or dupatta work too
  • Warm up for 2–3 minutesEasy walking or ankle circles
  • Move slowlyMild effort is fine; sharp pain is a stop sign

The routine

Five poses, in this order.

01Setu Bandhasana

Supported Bridge

  • 5 breaths
  • 8–10 lifts

Why: strengthens the glutes and hamstrings that take load off the knee.

Lie on your back, knees bent, feet hip-width and flat. Slide a block under your sacrum and rest for 5 slow breaths, then lift your hips off the block 8–10 times.

Knee-safe tip: Keep knees pointing straight ahead, not falling in or out.

02On your back

Reclined Straight-Leg Raise

  • 10 reps each side

Why: builds the quadriceps without bending the knee.

Lie on your back, one knee bent, the other leg straight. Tighten the front thigh and lift the straight leg to the height of the bent knee.

Knee-safe tip: Keep your lower back gently pressed down.

03Supta Padangusthasana

Reclined Hamstring Stretch with Strap

  • 30–45 sec each side

Why: releases tight hamstrings that pull on the back of the knee.

Lie down, loop a strap around one foot and lift the leg until you feel a gentle stretch.

Knee-safe tip: A micro-bend in the knee is fine; never lock it.

04Utkatasana, shallow

Mini Chair Pose

  • 3–5 breaths
  • 3 rounds

Why: strengthens the thighs in a weight-bearing position.

Stand with feet hip-width. Sit your hips back a few inches as if toward a chair, arms forward.

Knee-safe tip: Knees track over the second toes and stay behind the toes. Keep it shallow.

05Vrksasana

Tree Pose

  • 5–8 breaths each side

Why: improves balance and stability around the knee.

Stand tall and place one foot on the inner ankle or calf, never on the side of the knee. Hands at heart; use a wall if needed.

Knee-safe tip: Press foot and leg into each other for steadiness.

How often

A few short sessions a week, built up slowly.

Knee-safety rules

Gentle means pain-free, every time.

In every practice

  • Stop if you feel sharp, pinching or worsening pain.
  • Pad your knees whenever kneeling.
  • Knees track over the second toes in every standing pose.

Wait for the all-clear

  • Avoid deep kneeling, full squats and Hero Pose until cleared by a professional.
  • Swelling, locking, giving way or pain after an injury: see a doctor or physiotherapist first.
Not medical advice. This is general guidance. If you've had surgery, an injury or an arthritis flare, check with your doctor or physiotherapist before starting.
